Why do some features need an account?

Student-only

Sign-up requires a college email, so everyone you trade with is a fellow student.

Accountability

Every listing, message and review is tied to a real profile with a rating history.

Less spam

Guests can't flood sellers with messages or fake reviews.

Moderation

Reports come from verified students; 3 reports auto-hide a listing until an admin reviews it.
